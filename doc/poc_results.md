# Proof of concept: day-ahead electricity demand at NHS sites

Generated by `sense-energy run-poc` (config `code/configs/experiments/poc.yaml`);
figures from `code/scripts/poc/`. First pass, 14 September 2026.

## Set-up

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | electricity, **site** level, kWh per half hour; scored on observed values only |
| Sites | **91** of 134 (≥ 85 % observed in both train and test windows, ≥ 1 year of history, not heavily imputed) |
| Issue time | **16:30 local on D−1**; forecast the 48 half-hours of D (lead 7.5–31 h) |
| Known at issue | demand up to 16:30 D−1 (no settlement lag assumed); Agile prices for D; NESO day-ahead ND and embedded forecasts; the **00z IFS ENS run of D−1** for forecast weather |
| Train / test | train ≤ 31 May 2025; test 1 Jun 2025 → 8 May 2026, one origin every 5 days (69 origins, all weekdays represented) |
| Baselines | seasonal naive (same half-hour one week earlier); profile quantiles (last 8 same-weekday/slot values) |
| SARIMA | dynamic harmonic regression per site: log demand = 3 weekly Fourier harmonics + SARIMA(1,0,1)(0,1,1)₄₈ errors, re-estimated at every origin on the 6-week context (warm-started from a fit on the last 8 training weeks), gaps left to the Kalman filter, Gaussian quantiles on the log scale; 6,279 fits on 36 cores |
| Zero-shot | Chronos-2, TimesFM 3.0: 6-week context up to the issue time, no covariates, quantile heads |
| Foundation models + covariates | the same contexts plus the known-ahead covariates as past/future series: local calendar (half-hour, weekday, weekend, bank holiday), the site's Agile price, NESO day-ahead ND/embedded/peak forecasts, and weather (2 m temperature, dewpoint, wind): ERA5 over the context in both variants, ahead either ERA5 (**+ ERA5**, perfect weather) or the ensemble mean of the 00z IFS ENS run of D−1 (**+ IFS ENS**, forecast weather). Chronos-2 takes them natively; TimesFM 3.0 as standardised past+future covariate rows; TabPFN-TS as extra columns |
| TabPFN-TS | TabPFN v3 time-series checkpoint on the published TabPFN-TS feature table (running index, calendar sine/cosine terms, auto-detected seasonal terms; 42 features, 56 with covariates), one regression per site and origin (8 estimators) |
| LightGBM | global direct multi-horizon quantile models (q10/q50/q90) on calendar, lags/aggregates ending at the origin, price, NESO day-ahead covariates, site statics; three weather variants: none / ERA5 at target time (perfect weather, upper bound) / IFS ENS control + ensemble mean/spread |
| Metrics | MAE, normalised MAE (MAE / site mean), CRPS approximated on the 9-quantile grid, 80 % coverage; skill = 1 − metric/metric(seasonal naive), median across sites |

## Results (pooled over 91 sites, 69 origins)

| Candidate method | nMAE (%) | MAE (kWh/½h) | CRPS | Coverage (%, target 80) | MAE skill (%) | CRPS skill (%) | Sites beating naive (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seasonal naive | 18.5 | 12.93 | 12.93 | 4 | +0.0 | +0.0 | 0 |
| Profile quantiles | 18.3 | 12.17 | 9.99 | 60 | +5.0 | +23.0 | 64 |
| LightGBM | 13.4 | 7.68 | 6.41 | 74 | +26.3 | +41.3 | 97 |
| LightGBM + ERA5 (perfect weather) | 12.8 | 7.38 | 6.16 | 73 | +29.5 | +44.1 | 97 |
| LightGBM + IFS ENS (forecast weather) | 13.1 | 7.50 | 6.27 | 73 | +28.7 | +42.6 | 97 |
| Chronos-2 (zero-shot) | 12.2 | 6.46 | 5.37 | 76 | +29.7 | +45.2 | 99 |
| TimesFM 3.0 (zero-shot) | 12.4 | 6.69 | 5.46 | 81 | +29.6 | +44.2 | 99 |

Seasonal naive is a point forecast; its "coverage" is undefined and its CRPS equals its MAE.

## Reading

- Every learned model beats seasonal naive by **~30 % MAE** and 41–45 % CRPS; the profile baseline barely does (5 %).
- **Zero-shot foundation models lead**: Chronos-2 nMAE 12.2 %, TimesFM 3.0 12.4 %, using demand history alone. TimesFM's 80 % band is calibrated (81 %); Chronos-2's is slightly narrow (76 %).
- **Weather helps LightGBM and forecast weather is nearly as good as perfect weather**: 13.4 % → 12.8 % with ERA5, 13.1 % with the IFS ENS forecast. The honest-vs-perfect gap (0.3 pt) is small at day-ahead range.
- LightGBM's bands are too narrow (73 %) and its top split feature is `site_code`, i.e. site identity rather than dynamics — a first cut, untuned.
- Error peaks at 21 h lead (≈ 13:30 on D, the daytime peak) for every method: it is the level of the working-day plateau, not the timing, that is hard.

## Caveats

- No settlement lag: half-hourly data assumed available at 16:30 D−1. Set `data_lag_periods: 48` to emulate next-day delivery.
- IFS ENS 6-hourly steps are linearly interpolated to half-hours; dewpoint/radiation exist only from March 2024 (the training window starts June 2023).
- CRPS is a 9-quantile approximation; LightGBM's three fitted quantiles are interpolated onto that grid, so its CRPS is slightly flattered.
- One test window (2025-26); no retraining inside it; origins every 5 days.
